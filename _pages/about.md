I am currently a Ph.D. student in **Computational and Data Sciences** at [Washington University in St. Louis](https://washu.edu/), advised by Prof. [Michael A. White](https://sites.wustl.edu/mwhite/), and my research field is  **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**. Before this, I received my Master of Science degree in **Computer Science** from [Georgetown University](https://www.georgetown.edu/) and received my Master of Science degree in **Physics** from [The Chinese University of Hong Kong](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/). I also completed my undergraduate studies at [Beijing Normal-Hong Kong Baptist University](https://uic.edu.cn/) and earned my Bachelor of Science (Honors) degree in **Computer Science and Technology** from [Hong Kong Baptist University](https://www.hkbu.edu.hk/).

My research interests include algorithms, theories, and applications of **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**. I focus on developing new AI algorithms and improving the performance of AI models. I also focus on how to apply AI models more effectively in various fields. At the same time, I am also very interested in physics. The following are my current research interests:

* Large Language Models (LLMs)
* World Models
* Recursive Self-Improvement
* Computer Vision
* Deep Reinforcement Learning
* AI for Science & Mathematics
* Neural Networks & Deep Learning
* AI for Genomics
* Security & Privacy & Anonymity
